Domain

Research lifecycle intelligence for academic projects

ResearchLens focuses on reducing uncertainty in undergraduate research by combining semantic analysis, trend exploration, supervisor matching, and governance workflows in one platform.

Literature Survey

The project investigates semantic similarity detection, academic trend analysis, graph-based recommendation, blockchain-inspired audit trails, and NLP-based progress summarization for research supervision.

Research Gap

Existing academic tools often support only one isolated task. Students still struggle to validate novelty, identify relevant supervisors, prove submission integrity, and summarize progress without switching between disconnected systems.

Research Problem

How can an AI-assisted platform help students identify research gaps and trends while supporting trustworthy academic workflows from topic submission to supervisor review?

Research Objectives

Build semantic gap detection, recommend supervisors through graph intelligence, preserve submission evidence through integrity governance, and assist supervisors with concise NLP-generated progress summaries.

Methodology

ResearchLens combines SBERT embeddings, FAISS vector search, GraphSAGE scoring, PostgreSQL-backed services, Sepolia evidence anchoring, Socket.IO collaboration, and a fine-tuned BART summarization service.

Technologies Used

React, Node.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, SBERT, FAISS, Graph Neural Networks, Web3.py, Solidity, SHA-256, BART, Gemini AI, and Socket.IO.

Platform Modules

Built around the real research workflow

01

Research Gap Detection

Analyzes proposals and academic text to surface semantic similarity, novelty signals, and related research directions.

02

Integrity & Governance

Maintains tamper-evident academic records using hash-linked ledger events and external Sepolia anchoring.

03

Supervisor Recommendation

Matches students with supervisors using research interests, collaboration networks, workload, and GNN similarity scoring.

04

Workspace Assistant

Supports logbooks, group chat, GitHub analytics, milestone tracking, and AI-assisted supervisor feedback.
